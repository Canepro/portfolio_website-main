# Writing and topics

## Reach

Open `/blog`; choose a Topic, or open an article heading.

## Drive

Select the first nonempty option in the control labelled `Topic`; require matching `?tag=` and at least one `main h2 a[href^="/blog/"]`. Reload `/blog` and click the first article heading.

## Observable success

Filter URL changes and an article remains reachable; article route and visible heading render. HTTP contracts check discovered article routes and latest-article metadata.

## Prerequisites and limits

A nonempty topic and published article are prerequisites. The sweep does not compare every returned article tag, test empty topics, or check filter history/persistence; verify those if changed.
